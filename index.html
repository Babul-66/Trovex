FAQ

Questions, answered.

Anything else? Our support team is in the app, around the clock.

Do I have to drive myself?

No. Every search has a “with driver” switch. Turn it on to see only vehicles that come with a driver, or leave it off for self-drive.

Can I rent for just a few hours?

Yes. Choose an hourly rental with a pickup and drop-off time, or a daily rental for longer trips.

What’s included in the price?

The breakdown lists the base fare, 18% GST, any extras you add and the delivery fee if you choose doorstep delivery. A refundable security deposit is shown separately and collected at pickup.

What happens if I cancel?

The app shows your estimated refund before you confirm the cancellation. Cancellation charges are set per vehicle, so check the listing. Once a trip has started it can no longer be refunded.

How does the female-driver option work?

Women booking with a driver can pick “Only female drivers”, and they’ll be matched only with women drivers.

How do I list my vehicle or shop?

Open the Host & Earn tab in the app. List a single vehicle as an individual, or register your shop to manage a fleet. Our team reviews every listing before it goes live.
